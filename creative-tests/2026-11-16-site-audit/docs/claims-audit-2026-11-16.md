# EverLiv product copy and claims audit (Marketing, Nov 16, 2026)

## Scope and method
- **What was checked:** 43 live product listings from `everliv.life/products.json`, plus each rendered product page, fetched at 04:02 UTC on Nov 16 (11:02 PM ET, Nov 15).
- **How:** every description was scanned automatically for disease, treatment, certification, origin, efficacy, absolute and pricing language, with the standard FDA disclaimer sentences excluded. The flagged sentences were then read and classified by hand.
- **Evidence base:**
  - the Supliful label file and reply of Nov 15
  - the supplier front renders and flat labels in the library
  - the Nov 14 claims checklists
- **What we don't have:** no substantiation file is on record for any product.
- **Limits:**
  - I did not verify supplement-facts panels against Supliful formulas beyond the Eye Serum label.
  - FAQs, subscription terms and SEO were reviewed from the rendered pages.
  - This is a marketing risk screen, not legal advice.

**Classification key:**
- **V** Verified: directly supported by supplier documentation or approved internal evidence.
- **S** Needs substantiation: plausible but not documented.
- **M** Potentially misleading: inconsistent with the verified product, packaging or terms.
- **L** Requires legal review: medical, regulatory, certification, hormone or other higher-risk claims.

## 5. Coffee packaging and descriptions
- Every coffee main image is a **kraft gable-top carton with a clear valve bag of beans beside it**. Our editorial images show the carton alone.
- **Supliful has not confirmed which the customer receives.** Gino said packaging "depends on the order", and the help article covers shipping only.
- Until confirmed: avoid any copy that describes the pack ("resealable bag", "gift box"); describe only the contents ("12 oz / 340 g, whole bean or ground per label").
- The Mushroom main image shows **ground** coffee. Check the label's bean/form field for each coffee so the images match the form.
