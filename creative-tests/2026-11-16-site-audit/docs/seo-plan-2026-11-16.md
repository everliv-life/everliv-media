# EverLiv SEO plan (Marketing draft, Nov 16, 2026)

**Status:** recommendations only. Restructuring owns implementation in Shopify. Nothing has been changed.

## Priority fixes (in order)
1. **Image alt text: High.** All 43 products have empty alt fields, so the theme outputs "title thumbnail N". Paste-ready main-image alt text is in the table below. Editorial images have their alt text in `library/editorial/ASSET-REGISTER.md`.
2. **Title pattern: Medium.** Standardize on `<Product> | EverLiv`.
   - Current titles duplicate the brand: "Name | EverLiv | EverLiv LLC".
   - The FAQ uses "FAQ | EverLiv | EverLiv LLC"; make it "FAQ | EverLiv".
   - For pages, use `<Page> | EverLiv`.
3. **Unique meta descriptions: Medium.** Eleven pages share the boilerplate "EverLiv products reflect our dedication…". Drafts are below.
4. **Product meta descriptions: Medium.** Most product descriptions are the first lines of the body copy, so they repeat the claims flagged in the claims audit. Regenerate them from the conservative copy after legal review, at 150–160 characters, factual: format, key ingredients, amount and count.
5. **Naming consistency: Medium.**
   - Use "EverGlo" (label) instead of "EverGlow" (SEO title).
   - Change the Slumber Soap handle `everliv-clean-forrest-soap` and its meta "Clean Forest Soap". Keep the handle, with a redirect if it is changed, and change the meta text to "Slumber Soap".
   - Replace the Collagen Strips body text "Beauty + Collagen Strips".
   - The Oat Milk Honey meta says "Milk Honey Soap".
6. **Testosterone & NAD+:** the product is active (owner, Nov 15). Make no change to the product or its URL. Only align its title format ("Testosterone & NAD+ Support | EverLiv") when the other titles are done. Its meta description repeats hormone claims, which is on the attorney list. *(Corrected: the earlier unpublish and redirect advice was based on a wrong "discontinued" assumption.)*
7. **404s and redirects: Low.**
   - `/pages/about-us` returns 404; the live page is `/pages/about`. Add a redirect.
   - `/collections/bundles` returns 404; add a redirect to `/collections/bundles-offers`.
8. **Collection intros: Medium.** Rewrite the "Shop by Need" intros without claims; drafts are below.
9. **Structured data: Low, later.** Do not emit Review or AggregateRating schema for fewer than 5 genuine reviews. Product schema with price and availability is fine.
