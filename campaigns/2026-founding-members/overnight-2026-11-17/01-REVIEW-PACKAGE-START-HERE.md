## 1. The campaign in one paragraph
EverLiv is deliberately **not** running one-day Black Friday markdowns. Instead it opens a year: **Founding Membership, $49.99 paid once for 12 months of member pricing** on eligible products (20% or 15%, depending on the product), with no automatic renewal. The price rises to $69.99 on February 1.

The campaign runs in six beats:
1. It **teases** a "little gold" new chapter (Nov 20–22).
2. It **reveals** the Member Price Club (Mon Nov 23).
3. It **contrasts one day vs. a year** over Black Friday and Cyber Monday (Nov 27–30).
4. It **earns attention with useful holiday gifting** (Dec 1–20).
5. It shifts to **routines and "start before January"** (Dec 21–31).
6. It closes with a **truthful founding-price countdown** to January 31.

The calculator content does something most brands won't: it shows **when membership is not worth it**. That's the premium, trustworthy version of this offer, and it fits EverLiv's "read the label" honesty.

**Underlying idea:** *Your wellness journey evolves. So does EverLiv.*

**Recommended spine headline:** **"Black Friday ends. Your membership doesn't."** It carries the reveal through to the countdown.

## 4. Offer accuracy guardrails applied everywhere
- **Required wording and disclosures:**
  - "20% or 15% off, depending on the product", never "most 20%" and never "everything"
  - "$49.99 through January 31, 2027 (ET); $69.99 from February 1, 2027"
  - 12 months, no automatic renewal, no renewal price promise
  - doesn't combine with Subscribe & Save, bundles or promo codes
  - "Not available to residents of CA or NC", stated as a disclosure and never described as enforced
- **Never claimed:**
  - an invented Black Friday discount or code
  - gift memberships, gift wrap or delivery-by dates
  - guaranteed savings
  - health claims
- **Product exclusions:** Sleep and Collagen strips are excluded (unavailable). Testosterone & NAD+ is kept out of campaign creative pending Quantus documentation; its homepage placement is untouched. The Eye Gel-Cream is excluded (label-name issue).
