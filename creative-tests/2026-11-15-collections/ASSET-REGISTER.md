## Label key
- **READY FOR PUBLIC USE:** packaging verified, claims cleared, visual QA passed. *Nothing qualifies yet.*
- **CREATIVELY APPROVED / PENDING VERIFICATION:** Jeff approved the image. Only supplier or claim confirmation remains.
- **QA PASSED / PENDING APPROVAL + VERIFICATION:** independent visual QA passed. Jeff's creative sign-off and supplier verification are still needed.
- **PROVISIONAL:** needs more creative refinement or QA.
- **BLOCKED:** can't be completed accurately with current information.
- **+ COFFEE FLAG:** coffee imagery is *provisional for public use* until Supliful confirms whether the customer receives the carton or the bag.
