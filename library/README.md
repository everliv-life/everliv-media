# EverLiv verified product-asset library

This library is the source for any recognizable EverLiv product in social creative. The full index (SKU, source, last-checked date, discrepancies and photos still needed) is the **Product assets** tab of the EverLiv Product & Claims Library + Content Calendar.

## Authenticity standard (owner-approved, Nov 14 2026)

1. **Use only authentic source imagery.** Every recognizable product comes from the files in this library. These are supplier (Supliful) renders with EverLiv's production labels, as published on the live product pages, or verified photographs of real units.
2. **AI never draws packaging.** AI may make backgrounds, environments and editorial illustrations. It never draws, redraws, warps or relabels packaging.
3. **Composite cleanly.** Place the whole cutout or photo unaltered. The only permitted changes are uniform exposure and colour, scale, and natural shadows. No perspective warping, relighting maps or retouching of label art.
4. **No label edits to hide claims.** Never edit a label to hide or replace printed claims. Use a crop, a different angle or a different image instead.
5. **Exclude storefront infographics.** The infographic gallery images on the store (index 02 for most products) carry structure/function claims. They are not in this library and must not be used.
6. **Inspect before review.** Check every product at full resolution and at phone size before it goes for approval. If a convincing composite is not possible, use a plain product image or a product-free scene.

## Product presentation (owner standard, Nov 14 2026)
- **Hero:** when the product is the subject, show the complete authentic container (cap, label, base) with breathing room. If it doesn't fit, scale it down or rebalance the layout. Never cut it off.
- **Label education:** use a deliberate, clearly labeled close-up of the facts panel or label, designed as a detail inset, not a cropped product.
- **No half-products:** no half-containers in rounded frames, and no crops that show only the brand or product name.
- **Fallback:** if the authentic assets don't work, use a typographic layout or request a better asset.
- **QC:** check every frame at full size and phone size. No overlapping or clipped text; disclosures readable.

## File naming
`library/<shopify-handle>/<handle>--<view>.<ext>`

- **Views:** front, supplement-facts, packet-and-strip, open-tin, stack, front-cutout (transparent PNG), and so on.
- **Sources:** all files were downloaded from https://everliv.life/products/<handle> on 2026-11-14. The Energy flat production label comes from the Supliful product page.
- **What the files are:** renders, not photographs. Real-unit photographs will be added from the Nov 16 samples.
