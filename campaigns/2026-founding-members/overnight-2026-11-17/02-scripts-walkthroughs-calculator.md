## 2. Cart and checkout walkthrough concepts (static and video)
These are genuine scenarios using live prices from the product pages (Nov 16). Net figures always include the fee.

| Scenario | Everyday | Member | Gross saving | Net after $49.99 | Honest message |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One mixed order:** NAD+, Pure Creatine Monohydrate, Hydrate Strips, Charcoal Soap | $150.96 | $123.56 | $27.40 | **−$22.59** | One order won't cover the fee |
| **Two of those orders** in 12 months | $301.92 | $247.12 | $54.80 | **+$4.81** | Two orders clear it |
| **Three of those orders** | $452.88 | $370.68 | $82.20 | **+$32.21** | — |
| **Three orders:** NAD+, Hydrate Strips, Pure Creatine | $389.91 | $317.16 | $72.75 | **+$22.76** | Rendered as C09 |
| **Monthly Energy Strips** (12 tins) | $419.88 | $335.88 | $84.00 | **+$34.01** | But Subscribe & Save saves **$63.00** with no fee, so S&S wins for one fixed product |
| **Occasional soap buyer** (8 bars a year) | $167.92 | $142.72 | $25.20 | **−$24.79** | Everyday pricing suits them better |
| **Longevity pair every other month** (NAD+ + β-NMN, 6×) | $659.88 | $527.88 | $132.00 | **+$82.01** | Strong case. Use only with no benefit claims |

**What this tells us:** membership has a real but specific value. It wins for **variety buyers** who place **two or more orders a year** and for higher-priced 20% items. It doesn't win against Subscribe & Save for a single product bought on a schedule.

Leading with that honesty is the premium position, and it's consistent with "we didn't do a Black Friday sale". The C04 asset makes this point.

### 3b. Interactive website calculator: concept only (**not built or live**; needs approval and Restructuring)
**Placement:** `/pages/membership`, below "Example member prices", as an "Is membership worth it for me?" section.

**Inputs:**
1. **Product picker:** search the catalog and add items with a quantity per year. The picker reads each product's everyday price and member rate (20%, 15% or none) from Shopify data, never hard-coded.
2. Optional toggle: "I'd use Subscribe & Save for this item instead" (where S&S is available), to compare.

**Outputs:**
- Yearly everyday total
- Yearly member total
- **Gross saving**
- **− $49.99 fee** (or $69.99 from Feb 1, read from the live membership price)
- **= Net saving**, which may be negative
- One plain-language verdict:
  - "Membership likely pays off for you" (net > 0)
  - "Close call" (−$10 to +$10)
  - "Everyday prices or Subscribe & Save may suit you better" (net < −$10)
- Footnote: "Estimates from current prices; your cart shows your exact price. Savings aren't guaranteed. Excluded items (e.g. Pure Whey Protein Isolate, bundles) don't receive member pricing. Not available in CA or NC."

**Guardrails:**
- Never show a gross saving without the fee line.
- Never pre-fill a cart that implies typical usage or results.
- Excluded products show "no member price".

**Measurement:** events `calc_open`, `calc_add_item`, `calc_result_positive`, `calc_join_click`. These need Restructuring to implement.
