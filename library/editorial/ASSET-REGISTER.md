This register is generated from `registry.json` in the same folder. Every file has the disclosure **"Product shown as supplied; scene digitally created."** written into its PNG metadata.

**No asset is READY FOR PUBLIC USE.**

What changed with the supplier's reply:
- **Label finish is now verified.** Every Supliful label is high-gloss polypropylene (BOPP); matte, metallic, foil and pearlescent finishes aren't offered. The gold corner on the soap band is printed, not foil.
- **We may use photos showing our labels** (supplier-confirmed).

What still blocks public use:
- **Soap:** whether the supplier renders match the physical bars. No photos exist beyond the account renders, so this needs a sample order.
- **Coffee:** carton or bag. The supplier says packaging depends on the order, which doesn't answer it. Carton dimensions are also unverified.

**Visual approximation:** the supplier renders show the labels with low sheen under soft light. The real labels are high-gloss film. We do not paint gloss onto authentic render pixels. The exact sheen needs photography of physical samples.

Visual QA passing is not enough on its own.

File naming: `<product-or-collection>--<scene>[--format]--<role>-<W>x<H>.png`. The roles are master, social 1080x1350, mobile-social 1080x1350, desktop 2560x1429 and 1920x1072, and listing 1080x1350.
